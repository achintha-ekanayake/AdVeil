Overlay Ad Blocker - dev fixture page (not shipped with the extension)

Sample article content

This page exists only to manually verify the overlay heuristic engine (content-scripts/overlay-engine.js) during development. It is not referenced by manifest.json and is never loaded by the packed extension.

Load this extension unpacked, then open this file (via a local server or file:// with "Allow access to file URLs" enabled), and open the console to watch fixtures appear over the next ~2-10 seconds.

What should happen

  • Positive #1 - a full-screen "disable your ad blocker" fake overlay appears after 2s with a body scroll-lock. Should be hidden by the heuristic engine.
  • Positive #2 - a sticky bottom banner (#ad-banner-sticky) appears after 2.5s. Mid-range score - useful for threshold tuning; may or may not clear the bar depending on OAB_HIDE_THRESHOLD.
  • Negative controls 1-3 - a login-style modal, a cookie-consent banner, and a paywall strip are all present from page load, with no ad-keyword classes and no scroll-lock. These must remain visible - if any disappear, the heuristic is over-triggering.
  • Negative control 4 - an accessible modal drawer (role="dialog", aria-modal="true") appears after 3s with a body scroll-lock, full-viewport fixed wrapper, and z-index 1300 - deliberately reproducing every signal a real anti-adblock nag has (see Finding 11: the MUI docs "Settings" drawer false positive). Must remain visible.
  • Negative control 5 - a Select-style dropdown (role="listbox", wrapped in a plain role="presentation" positioning div) appears after 3.5s with the same fixed/coverage/delayed signals (see Finding 12: Cloudflare's Kumo-design-system Select). Must remain visible.
  • Negative control 6 - an empty, aria-hidden="true" full-viewport scrim (no text content) appears after 4s (see Finding 13: Base UI's inert popup/drawer backdrop). Must remain visible.
  • Negative control 7 - a bottom drawer with role="dialog" but no aria-modal, scroll-locked, appears after 4.5s (see Finding 14: Cloudflare's "Connect to a repository" drawer). Must remain visible.
  • Negative control 8 - a Radix-style profile dropdown (role="menu" inside a bare position: fixed popper wrapper with no role of its own) appears after 5s with a scroll-lock, reproducing the position(3) + scrollLock(3) + delayed(1) combination every modal dropdown menu produces (see Finding 15: HackerRank's profile dropdown). Must remain visible.

Set OAB_DEBUG = true at the top of overlay-engine.js to see the per-signal score breakdown for every evaluated element in the console.
